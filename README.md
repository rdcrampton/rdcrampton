# Hey, I'm Reg

I'm the founder of **Aelira AI** and maintainer of **[Aelira Core](https://github.com/Aelira-AI/aelira-core)**. I'm a full-stack developer working on AI and accessibility, with a focus on higher education. The web and the materials people learn from should work for everyone.

## What I'm building

[Aelira Core](https://github.com/Aelira-AI/aelira-core) is our free, open-source, self-hostable accessibility platform. It brings together scanning and supported remediation for documents, LMS course content, web pages and media, with reports and human review.

The core is in beta and under active validation. It can apply eligible changes and produce review candidates; unsupported or ambiguous cases remain unresolved. Automated scanner results do not establish accessibility conformance, and saved outputs need review before publication.

## Research direction

I want to understand when AI actually helps people do accessibility remediation well, and where it introduces errors or extra checking work. The research I am seeking to undertake compares generalist agents with specialised workflows, then tests what further training of an open-weight model adds.

Our longer-term goal is an accessibility specialist model. The immediate work is evaluation, source fidelity and practical safeguards, with openly released tools and findings as the aim.
